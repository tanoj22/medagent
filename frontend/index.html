The problem

Answering one research question usually means three tabs.

PubMed for the literature, a cheminformatics tool for the molecule, a protein database for the sequence. MedAgent collapses that into a single prompt, and unlike a plain chatbot, it computes real numbers with RDKit and Biopython and cites real papers, so you can trust what it says.
